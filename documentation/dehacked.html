Scope

This documentation is haphazardly thrown together as a way for me to learn DeHackEd. It is by no means comprehensive and only exists to benefit my Doom wad development. It is compiled from various online sources, as well as my own experience with working in the language. I mainly develop wads for Doom Retro, which supports the MBF21 format as well as having a few source port exclusive features. I will try to clearly indicate the exclusivity of any feature.
